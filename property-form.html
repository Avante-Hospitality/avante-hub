Property Owner / Affiliate Onboarding

List a property for short-term leisure rental, one step at a time. Fields marked * are required. Your submission is reviewed by Avante Travel before it goes live — nothing is published automatically.

1. Start

Read the terms, say who is submitting, and — if the property is already on Airbnb, Booking.com or LekkeSlaap — paste screenshots of its listing. Add any Word, Excel (rates) or PDF documents the property has. The form fills itself in from them, and you check each step.

Before You Start

Every listing is governed by the Avante Hospitality Terms & Conditions. Please read them — you'll confirm you accept them in the last step.

4. Property details

Use the same name, description and times the property shows on its other channels, so guests see one property everywhere.

5. Photos & documents

Upload the property's own photos (the originals, not copies from a channel), and the documents Avante needs before a listing can go live.

Property Photos

Photos of the whole property: the outside, garden, pool, shared areas, views and the area. Upload your own, or pick them from the Avante photo library. Photos of a unit type (its rooms, bathroom, kitchen) go in the next step, under that unit type.

Documents already with this listing

Documents uploaded before. Tap one to open or download it. New ones you add below are kept as well.

Photos from Google

Area and resort photos Google has for this place — handy for the surroundings, pool or beach. Each one shows who took it; keep that credit when it's used.

Compliance Documents

Clear photos or scans are fine (image or PDF, max 10MB each). Required before a listing can go live.

7. Calendar & rates

Nothing to fill in here. Your rates and availability come from your channel manager (for example NightsBridge) through Avante, so they stay there. Go on to step 8.
