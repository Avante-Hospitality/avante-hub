Property Owner / Affiliate Onboarding

List a property for short-term leisure rental, one step at a time. Fields marked * are required. Your submission is reviewed by Avante Travel before it goes live — nothing is published automatically.

1. Start

Read the agreement, say who is submitting, and — if the property is already on Airbnb, Booking.com or LekkeSlaap — paste screenshots of its listing. Add any Word, Excel (rates) or PDF documents the property has. The form fills itself in from them, and you check each step.

Before You Start

Every listing is governed by the Short-Term Leisure Rental Listing & Mandate Agreement. Please read it — you'll confirm you accept it in the last step.
