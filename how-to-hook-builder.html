🚪 Open the hook builder

  1. Log in to the hub.
  2. Affiliates: go to Affiliate Marketing and tap Open the hook builder. Admin: go to Default Hooks and tap Open the hook builder.
  3. It opens in a new tab, already signed in. Top right says "Signed in as …".
You stay signed in for 12 hours. A yellow bar "Your hook builder login has run out" means: go back to the hub and open it again. No button in the hub? Ask Avante Travel to switch the hook builder on for you.

At the top are 4 tabs: Hooks Photo library Checks Settings & backup. Everything saves by itself: top right first says "Saved in this browser", then "Saved to your account" a few seconds later.

Just after it opens, top right says "Getting your hooks from your account…". Wait a moment: you can look at your hooks, and change them once it says "Saved in this browser".

✨ Make a new hook

The builder opens step by step on a phone and on a computer. The steps below are the full view: tap ✕ at the top to see it.
  1. On Hooks, tap a tile that says Untitled hook · Empty, or + Add hook.
  2. Choose the flyer type: tap Property flyer, Event flyer or Area / town flyer.
  3. Step 3, Additional places to look for info: drop the event's flyer or screenshots (up to 6 JPG, PNG or PDF files), type its website, or paste text. Each file shows with a Remove button.
  4. Step 4, When and where: you can leave it for now. Fill in the hook fills in the dates, and for an event its town, from step 3.
  5. No flyer or website? Pick Flyer from and Flyer to yourself, type a place in Find a region, town, suburb or property and tap it. For a property flyer, tick one property. For a town flyer, tick one town or suburb.
  6. Want to see a property's rooms? Tap ▶ next to the property in the tree: its unit types show, each kind once, with how many it sleeps.
  7. A hook for one unit: on a property flyer, tap Hook this unit next to a unit type. The hook is then about that unit only: page 1 says how many it sleeps, page 2 shows only that unit, the photos are that unit's own, and the front store Book link opens on it. It shows ✓ This hook's unit. Tap it again to go back to the whole property. You can also choose it under Promote.
  8. Choose the unit before Fill in the hook. Already filled in? Fill it in again to get the unit's photos.
  9. Step 5: tap Fill in the hook. Wait while it reads step 3 and fills the dates, place, flyer, photos, caption and booking link.
  10. Property owners (Property Affiliate) making a hook for their own property: the booking link opens your own front store on the hook's dates. The guest sees the free units, taps Book this unit, and books and pays there, like any front store booking. (Your front store must be switched on.)
  11. Property flyers: Fill in the hook also gets this property's live prices for each unit type from Avante (a few seconds). The price on page 1 and the units list on the property page fill in when it's done. The Where to stay pages on event and area/town flyers get each property's price too. Under Booking link you see the prices it found for each unit type (prices only, no availability). While it works, a bar at the bottom says Getting live prices from Avante… with a timer; it shows ✓ Live prices loaded when done. Other hook types don't get live prices.
  12. Choose the look: tap Option A (photo circles, or blob photos for a town) or Option B — blended photos. Property flyers show their looks as pictures, 5 at a time (see Property flyer looks). Event flyers choose a theme instead (see Event flyers).
  13. Page 1: check every box. Orange boxes are empty: type in them.
  14. Facebook picture message (under the page 1 boxes): it starts on Paid ad with all three ticked. Change it to Private post or No message if you want.
  15. With Paid ad, tick what it says: See more, More info, Contact us (one, two or all three). The label says "tap the button below" with an arrow down to the ad's button.
  16. Tap Choose style next to Label style. Tap one of the 10 styles.
  17. Check the pages after page 1 the same way.
  18. Booking link: check the dates and tap Build booking link. Tap Test to try it.
  19. Admin only: Landing page link (under the booking link) fills in by itself when you publish the landing page. Affiliates don't see this box.
The Facebook picture message only goes on the Facebook picture (see the Facebook size box), never on the flyer or the front store. Private post says "More info, link in the caption above" with an arrow up. Changed it? Tap Publish again.
Guide me is on to start with: a "NEXT" bubble shows what to do next (tap Guide me: on to turn it off). When everything is done you see "Your hook is ready ✓". What you type always wins: the builder never types over it (unless you tick "Replace what I've already typed").

📱💻 Step by step: one screen at a time (phone and computer)

The hook builder shows one screen at a time, with big buttons. On a phone it fills the screen. On a computer it is on the left, and the flyer preview stays on the right and moves to the page you're working on. The first fill never stops to ask about empty boxes: you do those at the end.

  1. Tap the hook you want (or + Add hook). A hook with something in it shows a small picture of its page 1.
  2. Tap Start this hook (or Edit this hook). Clear this hook empties it.
  3. Choose the flyer type. Tap one. It moves on by itself.
  4. Event flyer? Choose the theme comes straight after. Tap one. It moves on by itself.
  5. Event flyer: first Add more info (a flyer, screenshots, a website or text). Tap Next ›. The builder reads it straight away (this can take up to a minute). When and where then only shows if the info didn't tell us the place or the dates.
  6. Property or town flyer: first When and where (pick the dates; tick the property or town — a property affiliate's own property is already filled in), then Add more info.
  7. Tap Flyer from (or Flyer to). One calendar opens: tap the first day, then the last day. Both dates fill in and the calendar closes. One day only? Tap the day, then Only this day.
  8. Tap Fill in the hook. Watch the bar at the bottom.
  9. Event flyers: if the event's name suits another theme (e.g. "Halloween"), the fill switches to it and tells you. Change it back under Choose the theme if you like.
  10. Property and town flyers: Choose the look. You see your own page 1 in each look. Tap one. It moves on by itself, and the other pages are filled in that look.
  11. The fill runs to the end without stopping. Anything it couldn't find stays empty for now.
  12. Subheadings fill themselves in, so they are not empty boxes. To change one, open its page in Edit the hook and tap one of the Options under the box, or type your own.
  13. Your hook is ready. It says what is still empty, page by page (e.g. Page 1: 2 empty · Page 2: 4 empty). An empty box is left off the flyer.
  14. Tap Fill in the empty boxes to do them now, Edit the pictures, or I'm done.
  15. Fill in the empty boxes: one box at a time. The flyer shows that box marked in orange (on a computer the flyer is on the left and the steps on the right; on a phone the flyer is at the top).
  16. A yellow note says what the box is for and why it matters.
  17. Type in the box (or pick from its list) and tap Next ›. Don't need it? Tap Leave empty. The flyer moves to the next box by itself.
  18. Tap Stop here to go back to Your hook is ready any time.
  19. Editing pictures: each picture shows on its own. Tap Google, Stock or Choose (the library). Adjust moves it. Then Next ›. Page 1's pictures come first. Tap Done with pictures to stop any time.
  20. You see Complete. Tap Show the flyer to see and download it.
  21. To change a finished hook later: tap it, then Edit this hook. If it still has empty boxes, you go through them first (as above). Then, or on the Complete screen, tap Edit the steps. Nothing is filled in again.
  22. Tap a step in the list (e.g. Page 1 — check the details). All its boxes open, with what was filled in. Delete it and type your own.
  23. Tap Done ✓ to go back to the list. Tap Done ✓ at the bottom of the list when you're finished.
If your flyer, screenshots or website couldn't be read, Your hook is ready says so in an orange box. Tap Try reading it again. If it still doesn't work, type the details in.
One tap: when a tap finishes a screen (a flyer type, a theme, a look, both dates and the place, a picture, a choice from a list), the builder moves on by itself. Tapped the wrong one? Tap Back. Next › is still there. Typing needs Next ›, because only you know when you've finished.
Amenity boxes are never in the empty boxes list: if a property has none, they stay empty. Fill them in under Edit the steps if you want.
On a computer the flyer and the steps each scroll on their own. As you go from box to box, the flyer scrolls to the next one.
Phone only, at the bottom: 👁 Preview shows the flyer on the page you're working on. Split screen keeps the flyer at the top while you work below.
✕ at the top opens the full view (all the steps on one page). Step by step (bottom left) goes back. Your phone and your computer each remember which one you chose.

🎃 Event flyers: themes for every affiliate

Every event flyer now works like the property affiliates' one: you pick a theme and the builder fills the rest.

  1. In Choose the flyer type, click Event flyer.
  2. Right under the flyer types, Choose the theme: Halloween, Sports, Festive holidays, Nature & birding, Food, wine & music, Bold or No theme. Click the one that fits.
  3. Add the event's flyer, screenshots or website under Additional places to look for info (step 3).
  4. In the Guide me bubble, click Done (or Skip — nothing extra).
  5. Click Fill in the hook. It fills the event's name, dates, town, highlights and the theme's photos on page 1.
  6. The pages after page 1 are in the same theme: first Where to stay (three properties a page, up to 3 pages), then Visits in the area (four places near the event).
  7. Change a property with the list at the top of its card. Change a visit with the Visit 1 to Visit 4 lists.
  8. Click Build booking link. The Book link works as before.
Page 1 is only about the event. The places to stay are on the pages after it and on the landing page. The button on every page says Book your stay: the event is the hook that takes people to the stays.

Page 1 of every event flyer

Every theme has the same page 1 layout, in the theme's own colours (Bold is black and orange). Everything fills itself in from the event's information:

  1. The name: all but the last word in serif capitals (PLETT), the last word in orange script (Rage).
  2. A short line about the event, and where it is next to the pin.
  3. The dates on the orange tag, with the kind of event under them (e.g. FESTIVAL).
  4. How long it runs in the orange pill (e.g. 7 days), worked out from the dates.
  5. What's on: up to three highlights, with ticks.
  6. The main photo at the top and two round photos.
Type over any box under On the flyer to change it. Each theme keeps its own colours.
Event flyers made before today pick the theme their name suggests (e.g. "Halloween" → Halloween), or No theme. Click another theme any time.

🏡 Property affiliates: hooks for your own property

If you are a property affiliate, every hook is about your own property, and every Book link goes to your property's calendar and booking form.

  1. In Choose the flyer type, click Property flyer or Event flyer. (There is no Area / town flyer for you.)
  2. Your property is filled in for you under When and where.
  3. It says "Your property isn't in the flyer list yet"? Avante Travel is still setting your property up. Pick the dates anyway: the property fills in by itself once it's ready.

🎨 Colours

Change the colours of the whole flyer: every page and the Facebook picture. Photos and the Avante logo stay as they are. Event themes (Halloween, Festive and so on) keep their own colours; an event with No theme can change.

  1. On Your hook is ready (or Complete), tap Change the colours. (Full view: Colours is above Choose the look.)
  2. Tap a colour set: The look's own, Avante, Seaside, Bush, Winelands, Festive, Black & gold or Clean white. The flyer changes and it moves on by itself.
  3. Want to try another? Tap Change the colours again.
  4. Or tap Make your own. Pick Background, Trims and Wording. The flyer changes as you move through the colour chart, so you see it straight away.
  5. Happy? Tap Use these. (Leave without tapping it and the flyer keeps its old colours.)
Background is the boxes and panels the writing sits on. Trims are the banners, the See more button, ticks, round labels, lines and coloured words. Wording is the writing.
Wording too close to the background is changed to white or black by itself, so the flyer can always be read.

🧹 Save, clear and remove

  • Save hook: saves online now, so it opens on any phone or computer.

Clearing or removing a hook here does not take it off the hub or take its landing page down.

📚 Photo library

  1. Tap Photo library.
  2. To add photos: first fill in What are the photos of? Pick the kind in the first list: Activity, Property, Unit type in a property, Complex, resort or estate, Suburb, Town, Region, Province, Country or Theme (generic photos for themed hooks).
  3. Pick which one in the next list (type in Type to narrow the list to find it faster). For a unit type, pick the property, then its unit type. Tap Use this.
  4. Then tap Avante photos or Google photos, or drop your own.
  5. Forgot? A box What is this photo of? comes up. Pick one, and the photos are saved with it. (The same box comes up when you drop photos on a hook that has no place yet.)
  6. To find photos: pick a place in Show photos for, or type in Name or tag contains (a photo's name, or a place, property or event it is tagged with, e.g. Plett Rage).
  7. Rename a photo by typing over its name. Tap + Add a tag on the photo to add what it is of. Everyone sees the change.
  8. A photo always keeps at least one tag. To fix a wrong one: add the right tag first, then tap × on the wrong one (only admin can take tags off).
  9. The count above the photos says how many have no tag yet. Tick Only photos with no tag yet to see them and tag them.
  10. Admin: tap Tag the photos with no tag. It tags every photo it can work out. For the rest, tap Remove the … photo(s) with no tag (photos on your hooks are kept).
  11. To get all of a property's Avante photos: pick the property in Show photos for, then tap Save them to the library (it shows when some aren't saved yet).
  12. Photos of a new property onboarded in the hub arrive here by themselves when Avante adds its photos. Type the property name in Name or tag contains to find them.
Each property's Avante photos belong to that property only. A property's photo is never used for another property, a town or an activity.

  • Booking links: Holiday builder page (site ID), used for booking links. Leave it as it is; the hub swaps in each affiliate's own page.
