🚪 Open the hook builder

  1. Log in to the hub.
  2. Affiliates: go to Affiliate Marketing and tap Open the hook builder. Admin: go to Default Hooks and tap Open the hook builder.
  3. It opens in a new tab, already signed in. Top right says "Signed in as …".
You stay signed in for 12 hours. A yellow bar "Your hook builder login has run out" means: go back to the hub and open it again. No button in the hub? Ask Avante Travel to switch the hook builder on for you.

At the top are 4 tabs: Hooks Photo library Checks Settings & backup. Everything saves by itself: top right first says "Saved in this browser", then "Saved to your account" a few seconds later.

✨ Make a new hook

  1. On Hooks, tap a tile that says Untitled hook · Empty, or + Add hook.
  2. Choose the flyer type: tap Property flyer, Event flyer or Area / town flyer.
  3. When: pick Flyer from and Flyer to (the dates on the flyer).
  4. Where: type a place in Find a region, town, suburb or property and tap it. For a property flyer, tick one property. For a town flyer, tick one town or suburb.
  5. Optional: add a flyer (JPG, PNG or PDF), a website, or paste some text, so the builder can copy facts from it.
  6. Tap Fill in the hook. Wait while it fills the flyer, photos, caption and booking link.
  7. Property flyers: Fill in the hook also gets this property's live prices for each unit type from StockNetwork (about 1 to 3 minutes). The price on page 1 and the units list on the property page fill in when it's done. Under Booking link you see the prices it found for each unit type (prices only, no availability). Other hook types don't get live prices.
  8. Choose the look: tap Option A (photo circles, or blob photos for a town) or Option B — blended photos. Property flyers also have Option C — lean, Facebook first (see below).
  9. Page 1: check every box. Orange boxes are empty: type in them.
  10. Facebook picture message (under the page 1 boxes): it starts on Paid ad with all three ticked. Change it to Private post or No message if you want.
  11. With Paid ad, tick what it says: Book now, More info, Contact us (one, two or all three). The label says "tap the button below" with an arrow down to the ad's button.
  12. Tap Choose style next to Label style. Tap one of the 10 styles.
  13. Check the pages after page 1 the same way.
  14. Booking link: check the dates and tap Build booking link. Tap Test to try it.
  15. Admin only: Landing page link (under the booking link) fills in by itself when you publish the landing page. Affiliates don't see this box.
The Facebook picture message only goes on the Facebook picture (see the Facebook size box), never on the flyer or the front store. Private post says "More info, link in the caption above" with an arrow up. Changed it? Tap Publish again.
Guide me is on to start with: a "NEXT" bubble shows what to do next (tap Guide me: on to turn it off). When everything is done you see "Your hook is ready ✓". What you type always wins: the builder never types over it (unless you tick "Replace what I've already typed").

🌅 Option C — lean, Facebook first (property flyers)

Page 1 carries one message: place, dates and price. It is already Facebook size, so the Facebook picture is page 1 itself.

  1. In Choose the look, tap Option C — lean, Facebook first.
  2. Check the page 1 boxes: PLACE LINE, HEADLINE, ONE SHORT SENTENCE.
  3. DATES (top right) come from step 3. UNDER THE DATES is a short line, e.g. Sleeps 8.
  4. PRICE BADGE shows the price a night, e.g. From R3 290 a night. Type over it for another price, e.g. From R415 pp a night.
  5. Check PROPERTY NAME and FACTS LINE.
  6. Photos: HERO PHOTO (the whole page, the beach or the area) and SMALL PHOTO (the property). Use Google, Change or Adjust position & zoom as always.
  7. Choose the Facebook picture message as before (Book now, More info, Contact us, and the 10 styles). On the flyer, the phone and email show in that spot instead.
  8. Pages 2 to 4 (the property, things to do, around town) fill in the same way as Option B. A faded area photo sits behind all of them.
Keep the headline specific: “Your family beach week” works better than “Summer special”.

🖼️ Photos

  1. Under Page 1 photos, tap StockNetwork photos of … (for a town: Hub photos of …), Google photos of … or Pick from library. (The first two show when one place is ticked.)
  2. Or search Google for anything: type in the search box (e.g. "Robberg Nature Reserve Plettenberg Bay") and tap Google photos.
  3. Or drop your own photos in the box (they also go into the shared library).
  4. In each photo box: Google (pick from Google), Choose (or Change; pick from the library). A filled box also has Clear.
  5. Tap Adjust position & zoom on a filled box to move or zoom the photo, then Done.
Up to 6 photos on page 1. The first one is the cover. On a property flyer, box 1 (the top photo) is the town the property is in: Fill in the hook puts a Google photo of the town there, and its Google button shows photos of the town.

Faded area photo behind the pages

  1. Open the first step after page 1 (e.g. Page 2 — things to do). Faded area photo behind the pages is at the top of it.
  2. It is on to start with, using the hook's own area photo. Untick Show a faded photo of the area behind the what to do and where to stay pages to switch it off.
  3. Pick How faded: Light, Medium or Strong.
  4. To use another photo: tap Google and pick a Google photo of the area, or tap Choose (or Change) and pick one from the library.
  5. Want the hook's own area photo back? Tap Use the hook's own.
The same photo goes behind every page after page 1, but not the resort page. Untick the box to take it away.

📤 Download and share

  1. Look at the preview on the right (on a phone: tap Preview).
  2. Tap Download image (page 1) or Download PDF (all pages). Under the preview, the Facebook size box shows what Facebook gets. Tap Download for Facebook for that picture (always 1080 × 1350).
  3. Tap Captions, then Copy Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp caption.

🌐 Publish the landing page

  1. In the preview column, find Landing page.
  2. Check the link name after go.avantetravel.co.za/l/ (it's filled in from the hook name, for example dunes-december). At least 3 letters, not used by anyone else. You can't change it later.
  3. Tap Publish landing page. (It only works once the hook has pages after page 1: fill in the hook first.)
  4. Tap Copy and share the link, or Open to look at it.
  5. Changed the hook later? Tap Publish again to update the same link.
After the end date, the landing page opens the front store instead.
Publishing also makes the Facebook picture (1080 × 1350, the shape phones show in full). The hub's Post to Facebook uses it. If it says the Facebook picture couldn't be made, tap Publish again.

🎠 Carousel ad

A Facebook ad with square cards that people swipe through. Each card has a picture, and under it a headline, a description and a Book now button.

  1. Publish the landing page first (see Publish the landing page). Every card's Book now goes to it.
  2. Fill in the hook, then open the last step: Carousel ad.
  3. Check the line Book now goes to:. It shows the landing page link. Tap Copy to copy it.
  4. Check Text above the cards. Keep the main message in the first 125 letters.
  5. Under Choose the look, tap the look you want: Option A — one photo or Option B — two photos blended. The chosen one has a ✓ and a thick border.
  6. Look at the preview on the right, under the pages (on a phone: under How it looks on Facebook). Tap ‹ and › to go through the cards.
  7. Under The cards, check each card: On the picture – big headline, On the picture – small line, Under the picture – headline and Under the picture – description.
  8. To move a photo or zoom in, tap Adjust position & zoom under it. Drag the photo, or use the arrows and the Zoom slider. Tap Done.
  9. Option B has two photo boxes per card: LEFT PHOTO and RIGHT PHOTO. Option A has one. Both photos are of the card's subject: the hook builder gets the second one from Google by itself (a bungee card gets two bungee photos). The offer and units cards use more photos of the property.
  10. Change a photo: tap Google to search Google, or Choose (Change) for the photo library. Clear takes it off.
  11. Want a dates tag or price tag on a card? Tap Add a dates tag or price tag and type in Dates tag or Price tag. Card 1 already has them.
  12. Don't want a card? Untick Use this card.
  13. Want another card? Tap Add a card. Fill in its boxes and photo. Remove card takes it away again.
  14. Tap Download the card pictures. You get one zip file with every card picture (1080 × 1080) and ad-text.txt with all the words and the link.
  15. Tap Download the preview page to see the ad on any computer or phone, without internet.
  16. To post it from the hub: tap Publish again on the landing page. That saves the cards. Then in the hub, tap Post to Facebook and choose Carousel — swipe cards.
Cards fill themselves in: card 1 is the offer (dates, price, place), card 2 the units, the next cards are things to do from the pages, and the last card asks for the booking. Area and event hooks get a where to stay card instead of the units.
Each box says typed once you change it. Your words stay. Fill this card in again puts one card back. Fill in all cards again puts every card back.
In Facebook Ads Manager choose Carousel. For each card upload its picture and copy its headline and description from ad-text.txt. Set the button to Book now and the link to the landing page link. Facebook needs 2 to 10 cards.

🏁 Put a hook on the hub (admin only)

  1. In Load to hub — Default Hook (under the landing page), tap a Default Hook N tile, or + New hook to add a new one.
  2. Tap Load to Default Hook N. It publishes the landing page first if needed.
  3. That slot already has something? Tap Replace it.
  4. Later changes: tap Publish again. The hub hook updates too.
Already on the hub? Then you see Update Default Hook N and Put it on a different Default Hook instead of the tiles. The hub hook's end date comes from "Flyer to" and is sent again with every Publish again. Set its place on front stores in admin › Default Hooks. Affiliates use their hooks on their own hub cards (Affiliate Marketing).

🧹 Save, clear and remove

  • Save hook: saves online now, so it opens on any phone or computer.
  • Clear this hook, then Clear hook: empties it (text, photos, links). Can't be undone unless you have a backup.
  • Remove hook: only on the last extra hook (7 and up).
Clearing or removing a hook here does not take it off the hub or take its landing page down.

📚 Photo library

  1. Tap Photo library.
  2. To add photos: pick a place in Tag new photos with, then tap StockNetwork / hub photos or Google photos, or drop your own.
  3. To find photos: pick a place in Show photos for, or type in Name contains.
  4. Rename a photo by typing over its name. Add or remove its places.
The library is shared by everyone. Only Avante admin can delete photos from it. (Find duplicate photos only tidies the copy in your own browser.)

✅ Checks

  1. Tap Checks.
  2. Read each list: collapsed booking/landing links, booking links to rebuild, check-in dates in the past, and hooks missing a photo or booking link.
  3. Tap Apply fix where offered, or tap a hook to open it and fix it.
"All clear." means nothing to fix. Rebuild all from property & dates rebuilds every booking link.
Clear all booking links empties the booking link on every hook.

⚙️ Settings & backup

  • Booking links: Holiday builder page (site ID), used for booking links. Leave it as it is; the hub swaps in each affiliate's own page.
  • Flyer contact info: the phone, email and name printed on flyers.
  • Your properties: loads by itself from the hub. Tap Load from the hub now if a new property is missing. Check the location tree gives a report.
  • Backup: Download backup saves all hooks and settings to a file. Restore from backup replaces everything on this computer with the file. Download hooks as CSV gives a spreadsheet.
Start over deletes all hooks, photos, settings and the resort list from this computer. Admin also has Clear the whole photo library, which empties the shared library for everyone.
