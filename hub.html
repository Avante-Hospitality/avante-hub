    How to Build a Great Link

    Turn your personal booking link into something clients actually want to click — in under a minute.

    1

    Start with the right tool

    Use Accommodation Link Builder for stays, or the ready-made "Your Flight Link", "Your Car Link" and "Your Free E-Sim Link" tabs for everything else. Your personal StockNetwork booking link is already saved here — you only ever paste it once.

    2

    Point it at something specific

    Don't send clients to a generic homepage — type a destination, or pick the exact resort from the dropdown. A link that already shows the place they're excited about converts far better than one that makes them start searching from scratch.

    3

    Set your dates and generate

    Add check-in and check-out dates if you have them, then click Generate link. This builds your personal, trackable booking link — every booking made through it is automatically credited to you.

    4

    Shorten it before you share it

    Long booking links look messy in a caption or bio. Click Shorten to turn it into a clean go.avantetravel.co.za link — still fully tracked back to you, just easier to read, type, and trust.

    The full flow, start to finish, recorded live in the Hub.
    Tip: If you're promoting a resort you've featured in a Reel or story, always use its own resort link — not a generic search link. Clients trust what they can already see.

    How to Add Your Storefront Ad

    Your storefront (the "Your Affiliate Storefront" link on the Home tab) shows up to 6 promo cards — each one is called a hook. Here's exactly how to set one up, and how to decide who keeps it fresh.

    1

    What a hook actually is

    Each hook is one promo card: an image, a booking link, and (optionally) a landing page link. All 6 hooks together make up your storefront grid — the page you share with clients so they can browse your current deals in one place. A hook with nothing filled in just doesn't show, so you're never stuck with empty cards.

    2

    Open Affiliate Marketing

    From the Hub menu, click Affiliate Marketing. Scroll past your storefront and embed links and you'll see 6 numbered hook cards — Hook 1 through Hook 6. Pick any empty one (or an existing one you want to update).

    3

    Add your booking link

    Paste the booking link for whatever you're promoting — a specific resort, a flight deal, anything with a StockNetwork booking URL. This is required. We automatically tag it with your affiliate ID so every booking made from this card is credited to you.

    4

    Add a landing page link (optional)

    If you've got a page with more detail on the deal — a blog post, a resort info page, anything — paste it here. It shows as a "See full details" button alongside "Book Now" on the card. Leave it blank if the booking link is all you need.

    5

    Upload the ad image

    Click Upload image and choose a photo for the card — a shot of the resort, the destination, anything that sells the deal at a glance. Use a portrait-orientation image (taller than it is wide) so it fills the card properly. JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 4MB.

    6

    Save it

    Click Save hook. You'll immediately see your booking link, landing link (if you added one), and a new client link — a single, shareable URL for just this one offer. That client link is what you send in a DM, WhatsApp message, or add to a bio when you want to promote one deal on its own, separate from your full storefront.

    7

    Decide who manages this hook

    Underneath the Save button is Who manages this hook? with two options:

    Manage my own — this hook stays exactly as you set it up, until you change it yourself.

    Let admin manage — this hook automatically shows whatever ad Avante Travel's admin has set as the current default for that hook slot. This is the default setting for every hook, so if you never touch this toggle, it's already switched on.

    You can set this individually for each of your 6 hooks — for example, keep Hook 1 as your own promo for a resort you love, while Hooks 2 through 6 stay on "Let admin manage" and update automatically whenever admin refreshes them. There's no need to check back — a change to the admin's version appears on your storefront right away.

    8

    What happens when your dates pass

    If you built your booking link with the Accommodation Link Builder and gave it check-in dates, a self-managed hook automatically switches over to the admin's default content once that check-in date arrives — so you never end up promoting an offer for dates that have already passed. You'll see a note under the toggle confirming this ("Your selected dates have passed, so this hook is automatically showing the admin's default content"). To promote a new date range yourself again, just update the hook with fresh dates and it switches back to your own content.

    Tip: A quick status line always sits under the toggle so you know exactly what's showing right now — "Currently showing your own content" or "Currently showing the admin's default content for this hook." If you're ever unsure why a card looks different than expected, check that line first.

    💬 Step-by-step: Sharing on WhatsApp Status & Broadcast Lists

    Every tap, from building the link to sending it — using The Dunes (a specific resort in Plettenberg Bay) and Plettenberg Bay (a whole area) as examples. No experience needed.

    Before you start: you'll need WhatsApp installed on your phone — it's free from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android). A Status update works just like an Instagram or Facebook Story: a photo, video or text update that shows at the top of your contacts' WhatsApp for 24 hours, then disappears on its own. A Broadcast List sends the same message to a group of contacts you choose, but each person gets it as their own individual message — not a group chat, and they can't see who else received it.
    1

    Decide: one resort, or a whole area?

    Promoting a specific property you have photos or a video of? Link straight to it — clients trust what they can already see. Not sure which resort someone wants, or just showcasing a destination in general? Link to the whole area instead, so they can browse everything available for those dates.

    2

    Build the link in Accommodation Link Builder

    In the Hub, click the Accommodation Link Builder tab. Both examples below use the exact same form — the only difference is what you type into one field.

    Specific resort

    Example: The Dunes

    1. Click into Destination or property name and type The Dunes, Plettenberg Bay. (If it happens to already be in Or pick a resort from the list, selecting it there does the same thing automatically.)
    2. Click into Check-in date and Check-out date and pick your dates, e.g. 12–19 Dec.
    3. Click the big Generate link button.
    Whole area

    Example: Plettenberg Bay

    1. Click into Destination or property name and type only Plettenberg Bay — no specific resort name. Leave the dropdown below it on — Select a resort —.
    2. Set the same Check-in / Check-out dates.
    3. Click Generate link — this link shows everything available in Plett for those dates, so clients can browse and pick.
    The specific-resort example — the Destination field with "The Dunes, Plettenberg Bay" typed in, and dates set to 12–19 Dec.
    The whole-area example — just "Plettenberg Bay", nothing more specific.

    After you click Generate link, a green/teal box appears underneath showing Your shareable link. It'll look long and messy — that's normal. Click the Shorten button next to it to turn it into a short, clean link.

    After clicking Shorten, your clean go.avantetravel.co.za link appears on the right — this is what you'll paste into WhatsApp.
    3

    Post it to your WhatsApp Status

    1. Open the WhatsApp app on your phone.
    2. Tap the Status tab (sometimes labelled "Updates") — it's one of the tabs along the top or bottom of the screen, next to Chats and Calls.
    3. Tap the round camera or pencil icon, usually in the bottom-right corner, to start a new update.
    4. Choose a photo or short video from your phone — a photo of the resort or destination works well. If you've made one, use the Done-For-You Canva templates on your Home tab.
    5. Tap the text box that appears over the photo, and type a short caption that includes your shortened link (copy it from the Hub, then paste it into WhatsApp).
    6. Tap the green or teal Send arrow to post. Your contacts will see it for the next 24 hours.

    4

    Send it to a Broadcast List

    1. In WhatsApp, go to the Chats tab.
    2. Tap the menu icon in the top corner — three dots (⋮) or three lines (≡), depending on your phone.
    3. Tap New broadcast.
    4. Tick the past clients or prospects who'd be interested in this destination, then confirm.
    5. In the new broadcast chat that opens, type the same caption and shortened link you used for your Status, then tap Send.

    Note: a broadcast only reaches contacts who already have your number saved in their own phone — if they haven't saved you, the message won't arrive.     5

    Follow up

    Under your posted Status, tap the small eye icon to see exactly who has viewed it, and how many times. Message anyone who viewed more than once, or who replies — that's usually genuine interest. A quick "Want me to check availability for your dates?" is often all it takes to turn a view into a booking.
