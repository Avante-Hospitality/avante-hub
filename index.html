SINCE 2004

Two decades of getting South African properties noticed.

Avante Hospitality was founded in 2004 by Jean Labuschagne, who's been developing and managing properties across South Africa and the rest of Africa since 1988. What started as a hands-on, personal way of getting properties into the right hands has grown into a booking platform of its own — without losing the personal touch that built it. Every property that lists with Avante is looked after by a small, dynamic team who know the local market, not a call centre. That's the difference between being one more listing on the open market, and being placed exactly where the right guests are already looking.
