🚀 First time? Start here

What you need: your affiliate number (Avante Travel sends it to you) and the password 0000.

1. Apply to become an affiliate

  1. Open the Become an Affiliate page Avante Travel gave you.
  2. Tap Agent Affiliate or Franchise Affiliate.
  3. Type your Full name, Email address and Phone / WhatsApp number.
  4. Fill in the other boxes if you like (where you live, how you will promote).
  5. Tap Submit application.
  6. Wait. Avante Travel contacts you and sends your affiliate number and your hub link.

2. Log in for the first time

  1. Open your hub link (or go to go.avantetravel.co.za/login.html).
  2. Type your affiliate number in Affiliate number (it may already be filled in).
  3. Type 0000 in Password.
  4. Tap Log In. Your hub opens.

3. Do these 3 things straight away

  1. Change your password: see Change your password.
  2. Add your email and bank details: see Account Details. Your email is how you get back in if you forget your password.
  3. Put the hub on your phone's home screen: see Add the hub to your home screen.
Your hub stays logged in on that phone or computer until you tap Log Out or change your password.

☰ Finding your way around

What it is: the round button with three lines (☰) at the top right opens the hub menu.

  1. Tap ☰ at the top right.
  2. Tap the screen you want, for example Accommodation Link Builder.
  3. The menu closes and that screen opens.

At the bottom of the menu you also find:

  • ❓ How-to Guide: opens this guide.
  • View Front Store: opens your own shop page in a new tab (see Your front store).
  • Change Password: see Change your password.
  • Log Out: logs you out on this phone or computer.
Every screen has a ❓ How to button at the top. Tap it to open the right part of this guide.

🔑 Change your password

  1. Tap ☰, then Change Password.
  2. Type your Current password (the first time it is 0000).
  3. Type a New password (at least 4 characters).
  4. Type it again in Confirm new password.
  5. Tap Save Password. You see "Password updated".
After changing your password, your other phones and computers will ask you to log in again (with the new password).

Forgot your password?

  1. Open the login page.
  2. Type your affiliate number in Affiliate number.
  3. Tap Forgot your password? and tap OK.
  4. Open the email from Avante Travel and tap the link (it works once, for 60 minutes).
  5. Type a new password twice (not 0000) and tap Save New Password.
  6. Log in with your new password.
The reset email only works if your email address is saved in Account Details. No email saved? Contact Avante Travel. At most 3 reset emails are sent per hour: if you tapped it more often, wait an hour.

📲 Add the hub to your home screen

Why: the hub then opens like an app, with one tap.

  1. Open the hub. A bar at the bottom says "Add your Hub to your Home Screen".
  2. Tap Add.
  3. iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Android: tap Install, or the browser menu ⋮, then Add to Home screen.
  5. Computer: click the install icon in the address bar, or the browser menu, then Install Avante Travel Hub.
Tapped ✕ by mistake? The bar comes back after 14 days, or use the browser menu steps above.

🏠 Home

What it's for: explains how the affiliate programme works and how much you earn.

  1. Tap ☰, then Home.
  2. Read the 6 cards: how you book, sell, get paid and what Avante does for you.
  3. Look at The Payout Math table to see what you earn each month.
  4. Tap Get your link to go to Affiliate Marketing.
The payout maths starts again every month.

📊 My Dashboard

What it's for: shows your bookings and where you stand on the leaderboard.

  1. Tap ☰, then My Dashboard.
  2. Pick a Category: All Categories, Accommodation, Flights, Activities, Car Hire or Packages.
  3. Pick a Period: Month to Date, Previous Month or Year to Date.
  4. Read your Rank (by value and by number of bookings).
  5. Read the rows Request, Booked, Cancelled, Confirmed, Paid: how many bookings and how much money each.
These numbers come from Stock Network reports that Avante Travel loads into the hub, so they are not live to the minute. No rank yet? It shows once you have a confirmed booking.

📣 Affiliate Marketing (your offers, called "hooks")

What it's for: your shop link, and the offers ("hooks") that show on your front store. Each offer is one card: Hook 1, Hook 2 and so on.

Copy your shop link (front store)

  1. Tap ☰, then Affiliate Marketing.
  2. At the top, find "Your affiliate storefront".
  3. Tap Copy. Paste it in WhatsApp, Facebook, your bio, anywhere.
  4. Want a short, neat link? Tap Shorten, then Copy next to the short link.
The Promotions embed link under it is the same shop without the header, for putting inside your own website. Every booking from your shop is credited to you.

Let Avante Travel run an offer for you (the easy way)

  1. Every hook starts on Let admin manage. Leave it like that.
  2. Avante Travel puts a ready-made offer on it, with your own booking link.
  3. You see a preview: the picture, "Booking link (personalized to you)", the landing page link and the caption.
  4. Nothing else to do. It shows on your front store by itself.

Hide an offer you don't want on your shop

  1. Find the hook card.
  2. Untick ☐ Show on my front store. It saves straight away.
  3. Tick it again to bring the offer back.

Make your own offer

  1. Find the hook card you want to use.
  2. Tap Manage my own. The edit boxes now appear (they are hidden until you do this).
  3. Picture: tap Upload image and choose a square picture (1080 × 1080 is best, 4 MB or less).
  4. Check the Booking link (it is already your own link) and the Landing page link (a web page, or tap 📄 Upload PDF instead).
  5. Want help filling it in? Tap ✨ Auto-build from property or area, type a place or property name, tap Generate, tap up to 4 photos, then tap Use this draft.
  6. Tap Save hook.
  7. Copy the Client link that appears and share it, or just share your shop link.
The links and caption only go live when you tap Save hook. (The picture, PDF, Manage my own / Let admin manage, Show on my front store, place and end date save the moment you change them.) A hook needs at least a booking link or a landing page link, starting with https://.

Make flyers, captions and landing pages (hook builder)

  1. At the top of Affiliate Marketing, find the Hook builder box.
  2. Tap Open the hook builder. It opens in a new tab, already signed in.
  3. Make your offer there (see the Hook builder guide).
No Hook builder box? Ask Avante Travel to switch it on for you. When it is on, the caption and flyer boxes on the hook cards are hidden, because you make those in the hook builder.

Choose where your own offer sits, and when it ends

  1. On a hook you manage yourself, find Place on my front store.
  2. Pick Rotates with the others (takes turns), or fix it: Fixed: level 1 (big, top), Fixed: level 2, left or right, or Fixed: level 3, left, middle or right.
  3. Pick the last day in Promotion ends.
  4. It saves by itself ("Saved.").
Your shop shows 6 offers at a time: 1 big, 2 medium, 3 small. When there are more offers than places, one place swaps to the next offer every 6 seconds (it pauses while a visitor points at or opens an offer). After the end day, your offer stops showing and Avante's offer for that hook number comes back. No end date? The check-out date of the booking link is used.

Give an offer back to Avante Travel

  1. Tap Let admin manage on the hook.
  2. Your own content is kept. Tap Manage my own later to get it back.

🛍️ Your front store (your shop page)

What it is: your own web page with your offers. Clients book from it and the booking is credited to you.

  1. Tap ☰, then View Front Store. Your shop opens in a new tab.
  2. At the top: Home, Book Your Accommodation, Flights, Car Hire and Free E-Sim, all with your link (Activities says coming soon).
  3. Under that: Holiday offers, 6 at a time. The big one is level 1.
  4. Each offer has Book these dates (or Check availability) and, if it has one, Full details.
  5. At the bottom: Search a place (towns from your offers) and Plan the rest of your trip.
You change what your shop shows in Affiliate Marketing. Changes show the next time the shop page is opened.

🏨 Accommodation Link Builder

What it's for: making a booking link for a place and dates, to share with clients.

Make a booking link

  1. Tap ☰, then Accommodation Link Builder.
  2. Your booking link URL is already filled in. Leave it.
  3. Under Where to, pick a Country.
  4. Pick a Province, then Region, City and Suburb. Stop at any level you like.
  5. For one property: after picking a City, pick it in Property.
  6. Check the line under the boxes, for example "South Africa › Western Cape › Garden Route › Plettenberg Bay".
  7. Pick the Check-in date and Check-out date.
  8. Tap Generate link.
  9. Tap Copy link and share it. Or tap Shorten first for a neat short link.
Tap Show preview to see what your client will see. Nothing picked under Where to? The link searches everything.

Send a client straight to one resort's info page

  1. Scroll to Resort Info Link.
  2. Pick the resort in Select a resort.
  3. Tap Generate resort link, then Copy link.

🗺️ Explore Map

What it's for: seeing properties and activities on a map, to help clients choose.

  1. Tap ☰, then Explore Map.
  2. Tap Properties or Activities at the top.
  3. Open the list: Country › Province › Region › City › Suburb. Tap a place.
  4. Picked a region, city, suburb or one property? Slide Within (for a region: Region plus) to choose how many km around it. The list shows what's nearby: activities around properties, or properties around activities.
  5. Tap a pin on the map for photos and details.
Know the name? Type it in Choose a property or activity by name. Tick or untick Properties, Activities and Region areas to change what the map shows.

🔗 Your Short Links

What it's for: every short go.avantetravel.co.za link you made, and how many clicks each one had.

  1. Tap ☰, then Your Short Links.
  2. Read each row: the short link, the long link, and "N clicks".
  3. Tap Copy to use a short link again.
  4. Tap Refresh to see the latest clicks.
Every tap on Shorten makes a new short link, even for the same long link. Re-use a short link from this list to keep all its clicks together.

👤 Account Details

What it's for: your contact and bank details, so Avante Travel can pay you.

  1. Tap ☰, then Account Details.
  2. Under Personal Details, type your Full name, Email address and Phone number.
  3. Under Bank Account Details, type your Bank name, Account holder name, Account number and Branch code, and pick the Account type.
  4. Tap Save Account Details. You see "Account details saved".
Lower down you can see your StockNetwork Site Nr and your Revenue Share (what you earn per category). Avante Travel sets these; contact them to change anything there.
Your email address is where the "forgot password" email goes. Keep it correct.

🏡 Property Affiliate

What it's for: owners and managers of a holiday property: calendar, bookings, guests, messages and channels (Airbnb, Booking.com, LekkeSlaap).

It has its own guide: Property affiliate how-to.

List a new property with Avante (onboarding form)

  1. Tap ☰, then Property Affiliate, then More ▾, then Onboarding Form.
  2. Tap View Terms & Mandate Agreement and read it.
  3. Choose I am the property Owner or I am an Affiliate/Agent acting under a signed Mandate.
  4. Fill in the owner details, the property details, policies and each unit type (tap + Add Unit Type for more).
  5. Tick the amenities and activities.
  6. Upload the documents (ID, proof of ownership, proof of residence, and the mandate for agents) and photos.
  7. Type your name under Signature & Agreement and tick the agreement box.
  8. Tap Submit Listing for Review. Avante Travel checks it and emails you.
