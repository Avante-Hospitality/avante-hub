Property Affiliate, step by step

For owners and managers of a holiday property. Your calendar, bookings, guests, messages and channels (Airbnb, Booking.com, LekkeSlaap), one small step at a time.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What's new

  • 10 Oct 2026 New look: a slim header and one tab bar (Calendar · Bookings · Guests · Communication · Channels · Reviews · More).
  • 10 Oct 2026 One calendar, right under the date search. Bookings show the guest's name. Tap a booking to open it.
  • 10 Oct 2026 The calendar can open on a set date, which is also your first bookable date (see Channels).
  • 10 Oct 2026 Cancel is now on every part of a booking.
  • 10 Oct 2026 Messages is now Communication: the hub emails guests for you, you can add up to 30 scheduled messages, and you can message guests who are staying now.

🚀 First time: connect your property

What you need: your property's own Stock Network API login: username, client ID and client secret. Avante Travel or Stock Network can give it to you.

  1. Log in to the hub and tap Property Affiliate in the menu ☰.
  2. Type your Username, Client ID and Client secret.
  3. Tap Connect.
  4. Asked "Which property is this?" Tap your property, or search for it by name and tap This one.
  5. Your calendar opens. Done!

Open it on another phone or computer

  1. Log in to the hub on that device. Property Affiliate opens by itself.
  2. If it asks, type your Hub password and tap Open ….
The hub keeps your Stock Network login locked away (encrypted). It only uses it to read your availability and make, change and cancel bookings for you.

Do these next

  1. Fill in Reservation settings and Guest payments: see Channels.
  2. Link Airbnb, Booking.com and LekkeSlaap: see Link your channels.
  3. Write your guest messages: see Communication.

📅 Calendar

What it's for: every unit, night by night. Tap a booking to open it. Tap open nights to book them.

Read the calendar

  • Teal "Open": free to sell.
  • Navy block with a name: a booking made in the hub. The name is the guest's.
  • Pink / blue / green block: an Airbnb / Booking.com / LekkeSlaap booking ("Airbnb guest" if there's no name yet).
  • Grey "Booked on SN": booked on Stock Network outside the hub.
  • Orange dashed: a channel booking not yet on Stock Network (see Channels).
  • Light grey dashed "Not on SN": nights you never gave to Stock Network.
  • Light grey dashed "Not bookable yet": nights before your calendar start date (see Channels).
  • Orange "Clash · Airbnb" (or another channel): a channel booking on nights already sold on Stock Network. Sort it out with the guest or channel.
  1. Tap › to see the next days, ‹ to go back.
  2. Tap Today (or Start date) to jump back to the start.
  3. Tap a booking block to open it (see Open a booking).

Find which units are free for some dates

  1. Pick the Check-in and Check-out dates at the top.
  2. Tap Search all units.
  3. Tap Select next to the unit you want. It goes into your selection at the bottom.

Make a booking

  1. Tap the first night on an open unit. It shows "Check-in" ("In" on a phone).
  2. Tap the last night of the stay. The nights turn navy ("Selected").
  3. Want more units on the same booking? Do the same on another unit.
  4. Tap Book this unit (or Book 2 units together) in the bar at the bottom.
  5. Type the guest's First name, Last name, Email and Cellphone (required). A name and a cellphone are both needed.
  6. Fill in adults, children, Booked through (Direct, Walk-in, …) and notes.
  7. Tap Make booking on Stock Network (for several units: Book N units on Stock Network).
  8. Send the guest the payment details: tap WhatsApp, or Email (if the guest has an email), under Payment.
The booking goes onto Stock Network as a Request. Your other channels are blocked for those nights within 15 minutes.

Find a booking

  1. Tap Find a booking.
  2. Type a reference, guest name, unit or channel.
  3. Tap Edit next to the booking.

Sell your open dates

  1. Scroll below the calendar to Sell these.
  2. Tap Copy booking link next to any open stretch.
  3. Or copy the ready-made message under Share your open dates with Copy message.

📂 Open a booking (Guest · Messages · Booking)

How: tap the booking on the calendar, or tap Open on the Bookings tab. It has 3 parts at the top.

Guest: the guest's details

  1. Tap Guest.
  2. Fill in name, cellphone, email, number of guests, arrival time, car registration and notes.
  3. Tick OK to send offers only if the guest agreed. Tick Opted out if they asked not to get offers.
  4. Tap Save guest details.
  5. When they arrive, tap Mark checked in.

Messages: talk to this guest

  1. Tap Messages.
  2. Each scheduled message shows when it goes out and if it was sent (WhatsApp and email).
  3. Tap WhatsApp to send it now on WhatsApp, or Email now to email it now. (WhatsApp only shows when the guest has a cellphone number, Email now only when they have an email address. Add them in the Guest part.)
  4. Your own message: type it in Write a message (and an Email subject), then tap Send on WhatsApp or Send by email.
  5. After check-out, tap Rate guest to review the guest.

Booking: change unit or dates

  1. Tap Booking.
  2. Change the Unit, Check-in or Check-out.
  3. Tap Save changes.
  4. Send the guest the new payment link.
Stock Network can't edit a booking. Changing the unit or dates cancels it and makes a new booking with a new reference. For several units: tap Remove unit to cancel one unit only.

Cancel a booking

  1. Open the booking (any part: Guest, Messages or Booking).
  2. Tap Cancel this booking at the bottom.
  3. Tap OK. It is cancelled on Stock Network and the nights open again.

Cancel a PAID booking

  1. Open the booking and tap Request cancellation.
  2. Tap OK.
  3. The affiliate linked to your property gets an email (tap Send by WhatsApp to send it to them on WhatsApp too).
  4. They open the link, type their hub password and tap Approve and cancel on Stock Network.
  5. Only then is it cancelled. Refunds are done outside the hub.
The approval link works once, for 24 hours. Too many wrong passwords block it: then request again. Property not linked to an affiliate? A paid booking can't be cancelled in the hub: cancel it in Stock Network.
Paid bookings are locked: they can't be changed in the hub. Changes go through Stock Network. Demo bookings can't be cancelled.

📋 Bookings

  1. Tap Bookings.
  2. Type in Search to find a reference, guest, unit or channel.
  3. Tap Open to open a booking.
  4. Tap Payment link to send the guest the payment details again.
  5. Paid by EFT into your account? Tap Mark paid (EFT), then also mark it paid in Stock Network.
Each booking shows its status (Request, Booked, Cancelled …) and payment (Unpaid, Part paid, Paid, Paid (EFT), Paid on channel). Payment link and Mark paid (EFT) don't show on paid, demo or channel bookings. Mark paid (EFT) also doesn't show when guests can only pay through the payment gateway.

Pay now (card or Instant EFT)

  1. After booking, tap Pay now (only there when guests may pay through the payment gateway). The secure Stock Network payment page opens in its own window.
  2. Pay there. When that window closes, the hub checks the payment.
  3. Not showing paid yet? Wait a minute and tap Check payment now.

Demo bookings (Property Testing only)

  1. On the Bookings tab, tap Load demo bookings to try everything with 6 example bookings.
  2. Tap Remove demo bookings when done. Demo bookings never go to Stock Network or your channels.

👥 Guests

What it's for: everyone who stayed or is booked, once each, for your records and return business.

  1. Tap Guests.
  2. Type in Search: a name, number, email or car.
  3. Tap Call, WhatsApp or Last booking.
  4. Tap Download (Excel) to save the list as a spreadsheet.
Only send offers to guests marked "OK for offers" and not opted out. Airbnb and Booking.com guests only if they agreed with you directly.

💬 Communication

How it works: Email is sent by the hub automatically, from your property's name, and guests' replies go to your email address. WhatsApp opens on your phone with the message ready, and you tap Send. (WhatsApp can also go out automatically with a paid WhatsApp Business setup. Ask Avante Travel.)

Write your messages (templates)

  1. Tap Communication and scroll to Scheduled messages.
  2. Tap a message (for example Welcome) to open it.
  3. Change the Message to your own words. Tap the grey chips like {first_name} to add the guest's name, dates, unit and so on. They fill themselves in for each guest.
  4. Choose When: Days before arrival, On arrival day, Days into the stay, Days before check-out, Days after check-out, or On a set date. Then the Days (or date) and the time.
  5. Tick Email, WhatsApp or both. Type an Email subject.
  6. Tick Switched on.
  7. Tap Save messages.

Add another message (optional)

  1. Tap + Add a message.
  2. Give it a Name (for example "Day 2 tips") and fill it in as above. You can have up to 30 messages.
  3. Tap Save messages.
Don't want a message any more? Open it and tap Remove this message, or untick Switched on. Then Save. "On a set date" messages go to guests staying on that date.

Send WhatsApps that are due

  1. An orange number on Communication means WhatsApps are waiting. You also get an email (sent to your Email on SN reservations address).
  2. Under WhatsApp to send, tap WhatsApp.
  3. Check the message, then tap Open WhatsApp and press Send in WhatsApp.
  4. Don't want to send it? Tap Skip.
Emails go out by themselves (the hub checks every 15 minutes). Emails that couldn't go out lists guests without an email address (tap Add details and the email goes on the next round) and emails that failed (the hub tries again).

Message guests who are staying now

  1. Under Staying now, tap Message in-house guests (or Message next to one guest).
  2. Tick the guests who must get it (tap Select all for everyone).
  3. Pick one of your templates in Start from a template, or write your own.
  4. Email: tap Email N guests. Each guest gets their own copy with their name.
  5. WhatsApp: tap WhatsApp next to each guest, one by one, and press Send in WhatsApp.
  6. Tap Done.
Set your email address first (Channels → Reservation settings) so guests' replies reach you.

⚙️ Channels: settings and payments

Reservation settings

  1. Tap Channels.
  2. Type your Email on SN reservations (also where guests' email replies go) and Phone on SN reservations (required).
  3. Type your Minimum stay (nights).
  4. Under Calendar opens on, choose Today, or A set date and pick the date.
  5. Optional: type a nightly channel price per unit.
  6. Tap Save reservation settings.
A set date is also your first bookable date: nobody can book in the hub before it, until that date has passed. Handy when you open for the season.

Guest payments

  1. Under How guests can pay, choose Both, Avante payment gateway only or EFT to our bank account only.
  2. For EFT, fill in your bank, account holder, account number, branch code, account type and (optional) a note for guests.
  3. Tap Save payment settings.
"EFT to our bank account only" needs your bank details first. An account number needs the bank name and account holder too.

🔁 Link Airbnb, Booking.com and LekkeSlaap

What it does: keeps every channel's calendar in step with Stock Network, so you never double-book.

  1. Tap Channels and scroll to Channel links per unit.
  2. For a unit and a channel, tap Copy next to Avante link for ….
  3. Paste that link into the unit's calendar import (sync) on Airbnb, Booking.com or LekkeSlaap.
  4. On that channel, copy the unit's own calendar export link (ends in .ics).
  5. Paste it in the hub box … 's calendar link.
  6. Do the same for every unit and channel.
  7. Tap Save settings and links. It turns "Connected" after the first read (within 15 minutes).

New channel bookings

  1. Scroll to Channel bookings not yet on Stock Network.
  2. Check the booking and tap Add to Stock Network.
  3. Happy that it works? Tick Book other channels' reservations onto Stock Network automatically and save.
Tap Check channels now to read the channels straight away. "Clash · sold on SN" means the nights were already sold: sort it out with the guest or channel.

"New unit on Stock Network"

  1. If a unit was only renamed, pick its old name under Renamed from and tap Move links to ….
  2. If it really is new, tap It's a new unit.

⭐ Reviews

  1. Ask guests for a review: switch on the After stay message in Communication. It includes their personal review link.
  2. Rate a guest: after check-out, open the booking → Messages → Rate guest.
Your Avante guest rating shows once you have 3 Avante reviews. Until then guests see your Google reviews.

➕ More: Onboarding Form, Hook Builder, Disconnect

  1. Tap More ▾ on the tab bar.
  2. Onboarding Form: list a property with Avante (see the hub guide).
  3. Hook Builder: make flyers and offers (see the hook builder guide).
  4. Disconnect property: stops the hub working for this property. Channel sync stops until you connect again.
