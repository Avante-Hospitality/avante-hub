🚀 First time: connect your property

What you need: your Avante login: username, client ID and client secret. Avante Travel gives it to you.

  1. Log in to the hub and tap Property Affiliate in the menu ☰.
  2. Type your Username, Client ID and Client secret.
  3. Tap Connect.
  4. Usually your calendar opens now. If you're asked "Which property is this?":
    1. Tap your property's name if you see it.
    2. Or type at least 3 letters of its name or town in Search by property name or town.
    3. Tap This one next to your property.
    Not found? Your property may not be set up with Avante yet. Ask Avante Travel.
  5. Your calendar opens. Done!

Open it on another phone or computer

  1. Log in to the hub on that device. Property Affiliate opens by itself.
  2. If it asks, type your Hub password and tap Open ….
The hub keeps your Avante login locked away (encrypted). It only uses it to read your availability and make, change and cancel bookings for you.

Do these next

  1. Fill in Reservation settings and Guest payments: see Channels.
  2. Link Airbnb, Booking.com and LekkeSlaap: see Link your channels.
  3. Write your guest messages: see Communication.

📅 Calendar

What it's for: every unit, night by night. Tap a booking to open it. Tap open nights to book them.

Read the calendar

  • Teal "Open": free to sell.
  • Navy block with a name: a booking made in the hub. The name is the guest's.
  • Pink / blue / green block: an Airbnb / Booking.com / LekkeSlaap booking ("Airbnb guest" if there's no name yet).
  • Grey "Booked elsewhere": booked with Avante outside the hub.
  • Orange dashed: a channel booking not yet with Avante (see Channels).
  • Light grey dashed "Not with Avante": nights you never loaded with Avante.
  • Light grey dashed "Not bookable yet": nights before your calendar start date (see Channels).
  • Orange "Clash · Airbnb" (or another channel): a channel booking on nights already sold. Sort it out with the guest or channel.
  1. Tap › to see the next days, ‹ to go back.
  2. Tap Today (or Start date) to jump back to the start.
  3. Tap a booking block to open it (see Open a booking).

Find which units are free for some dates

  1. Pick the Check-in and Check-out dates at the top.
  2. Tap Search all units.
  3. Tap Select next to the unit you want. It goes into your selection at the bottom.

Make a booking

  1. Tap the first night on an open unit. It shows "Check-in" ("In" on a phone).
  2. Tap the last night of the stay. The nights turn navy ("Selected").
  3. Want more units on the same booking? Do the same on another unit.
  4. Tap Book this unit (or Book 2 units together) in the bar at the bottom.
  5. Type the guest's First name, Last name, Email and Cellphone (required). A name and a cellphone are both needed.
  6. Fill in adults, children, Booked through (Direct, Walk-in, …) and notes.
  7. Tap Make booking (for several units: Book N units).
  8. Send the guest the payment details: tap WhatsApp, or Email (if the guest has an email), under Payment.
The booking goes onto Avante's booking system as a Request. Your other channels are blocked for those nights within 15 minutes.

Find a booking

  1. Tap Find a booking.
  2. Type a reference, guest name, unit or channel.
  3. Tap Edit next to the booking.

Sell your open dates

  1. Scroll below the calendar to Sell these.
  2. Tap Copy booking link next to any open stretch.
  3. Or copy the ready-made message under Share your open dates with Copy message.

📂 Open a booking (Guest · Messages · Booking)

How: tap the booking on the calendar, or tap Open on the Bookings tab. It has 3 parts at the top.

Guest: the guest's details

  1. Tap Guest.
  2. Fill in name, cellphone, email, number of guests, arrival time, car registration and notes.
  3. Tick OK to send offers only if the guest agreed. Tick Opted out if they asked not to get offers.
  4. Tap Save guest details.
  5. When they arrive, tap Mark checked in.

Messages: talk to this guest

  1. Tap Messages.
  2. Each scheduled message shows when it goes out and if it was sent (WhatsApp and email).
  3. Tap WhatsApp to send it now on WhatsApp, or Email now to email it now. (WhatsApp only shows when the guest has a cellphone number, Email now only when they have an email address. Add them in the Guest part.)
  4. Your own message: type it in Write a message (and an Email subject), then tap Send on WhatsApp or Send by email.
  5. After check-out, tap Rate guest to review the guest.

Booking: change unit or dates

  1. Tap Booking.
  2. Change the Unit, Check-in or Check-out.
  3. Tap Save changes.
  4. Send the guest the new payment link.
Avante's booking system can't edit a booking. Changing the unit or dates cancels it and makes a new booking with a new reference. For several units: tap Remove unit to cancel one unit only.

Cancel a booking

  1. Open the booking (any part: Guest, Messages or Booking).
  2. Tap Cancel this booking at the bottom.
  3. Tap OK. It is cancelled and the nights open again.

Cancel a Paid or Confirmed booking

  1. Open the booking. It says Paid: locked or Confirmed: locked.
  2. Tap Ask Avante to cancel.
  3. Tap OK. Avante gets an email.
  4. It is not cancelled yet. The status shows (Cancel requested) after it, for example Paid (Cancel requested).
  5. Avante cancels it and handles any refund. The status then changes to Cancelled.
Already cancelled it in your channel manager? Still tap Ask Avante to cancel, so Avante can update the booking.
Paid and Confirmed bookings are locked: they can't be changed in the hub. The guest contacts you, and Avante makes the change. Demo bookings can't be cancelled.

📋 Bookings

  1. Tap Bookings.
  2. Type in Search to find a reference, guest, unit or channel.
  3. Tap Open to open a booking.
  4. Tap Payment link to send the guest the payment details again.
  5. Money in your account? Tap Paid by EFT. Paid at the property? Tap Paid on arrival. The hub records the payment with Avante, which sets the booking to Paid. You don't have to do anything else.
Each booking shows its status (Request, Booked, Paid, Cancelled) and payment (Unpaid, Part paid, Paid, Paid (EFT), Paid on channel). Paid by EFT and Paid on arrival don't show on paid, demo or channel bookings, or when guests can only pay through the payment gateway.

How a booking's status is set

BookingStatus with Avante
Airbnb, Booking.com, LekkeSlaapBooked straight away (paid on the channel).
Paid by EFT or on arrivalBooked straight away. Paid when you tap Paid by EFT or Paid on arrival.
Paid by payment gatewayBooked. The gateway sets Paid. Unpaid ones are cancelled by Avante after the payment time.

Every 15 minutes, and whenever you open Bookings, the hub asks Avante what changed, so gateway payments and cancellations show here. Confirmed is never set by the hub.

Accept or decline a front store guest

  1. Tap Bookings. A request waiting for you shows Accept and Decline.
  2. Tap Accept. It becomes Booked and the guest gets the payment details by email.
  3. Or tap Decline, type a short reason if you like, and tap OK. It is cancelled and the guest gets a polite email.
If you don't answer, Avante cancels the request after a set number of days.

Pay now (card or Instant EFT)

  1. After booking, tap Pay now (only there when guests may pay through the payment gateway). Avante's secure payment page opens in its own window.
  2. Pay there. When that window closes, the hub checks the payment.
  3. Not showing paid yet? Wait a minute and tap Check payment now.

Demo bookings (Property Testing only)

  1. On the Bookings tab, tap Load demo bookings to try everything with 6 example bookings.
  2. Tap Remove demo bookings when done. Demo bookings never go to Avante's booking system or your channels.

👥 Guests

What it's for: everyone who stayed or is booked, once each, for your records and return business.

  1. Tap Guests.
  2. Type in Search: a name, number, email or car.
  3. Tap Call, WhatsApp or Last booking.
  4. Tap Download (Excel) to save the list as a spreadsheet.
Only send offers to guests marked "OK for offers" and not opted out. Airbnb and Booking.com guests only if they agreed with you directly.

🚤 Activities

What it's for: keep the activities you sell to guests, with the supplier's reference numbers, so anyone at the desk can book them.

Add an activity

  1. Tap Activities.
  2. Tap Add from nearby activities to see the hub's activities near you. Change Distance to look further.
  3. Tap Add next to one. Or tap Add activity to start from nothing.
  4. Paste the activity's booking page into Booking link. If it is an Activitar or ActivityBridge link, the hub fills in Booking system, Operator / supplier account and Product code / activity ID for you.
  5. Add Option / rate code, prices, Commission %, people and Runs on days.
  6. Add the supplier's Booking email so the hub can email booking requests. Several addresses? Separate them with a comma.
  7. Tap Save activity.
Activitar link: oceansafaris.activitar.com/services/575 → operator oceansafaris, product 575. ActivityBridge link: secure.activitybridge.com/book?activityid=12564 → activity ID 12564. Under "Missing:" the hub shows what still needs filling in.

Book an activity for a guest

  1. Tap Book an activity (or Book on an activity).
  2. Choose the Activity. The grey box shows its codes, days and rules.
  3. Fill in Date, Time, Adults, Children and the guest's details. Add the Stay booking ref to link it to their stay.
  4. Tap Make activity booking. It shows as Requested.
  5. Tap Book on Activitar / Book on ActivityBridge to book it on the operator's page with your agent login, or tap Email booking to supplier. It shows as Sent to supplier.
  6. When the supplier confirms, tap Confirmed by supplier and type their confirmation number.
Cancel only cancels it in the hub. Always tell the supplier too.
Ask each operator for an agent login on their Activitar or ActivityBridge booking page: you then see agent rates and can book on account.

💬 Communication

How it works: Email is sent by the hub automatically, from your property's name, and guests' replies go to your email address. WhatsApp opens on your phone with the message ready, and you tap Send. (WhatsApp can also go out automatically with a paid WhatsApp Business setup. Ask Avante Travel.)

Write your messages (templates)

  1. Tap Communication and scroll to Scheduled messages.
  2. Tap a message (for example Welcome) to open it.
  3. Change the Message to your own words. Tap the grey chips like {first_name} to add the guest's name, dates, unit and so on. They fill themselves in for each guest.
  4. Choose When: Days before arrival, On arrival day, Days into the stay, Days before check-out, Days after check-out, or On a set date. Then the Days (or date) and the time.
  5. Tick Email, WhatsApp or both. Type an Email subject.
  6. Tick Switched on.
  7. Tap Save messages.

Add another message (optional)

  1. Tap + Add a message.
  2. Give it a Name (for example "Day 2 tips") and fill it in as above. You can have up to 30 messages.
  3. Tap Save messages.
Don't want a message any more? Open it and tap Remove this message, or untick Switched on. Then Save. "On a set date" messages go to guests staying on that date.

Send WhatsApps that are due

  1. An orange number on Communication means WhatsApps are waiting. You also get an email (sent to the people you chose in Send Avante's emails to).
  2. Under WhatsApp to send, tap WhatsApp.
  3. Check the message, then tap Open WhatsApp and press Send in WhatsApp.
  4. Don't want to send it? Tap Skip.
Emails go out by themselves (the hub checks every 15 minutes). Emails that couldn't go out lists guests without an email address (tap Add details and the email goes on the next round) and emails that failed (the hub tries again).

Message guests who are staying now

  1. Under Staying now, tap Message in-house guests (or Message next to one guest).
  2. Tick the guests who must get it (tap Select all for everyone).
  3. Pick one of your templates in Start from a template, or write your own.
  4. Email: tap Email N guests. Each guest gets their own copy with their name.
  5. WhatsApp: tap WhatsApp next to each guest, one by one, and press Send in WhatsApp.
  6. Tap Done.
Set your email address first (Channels → Reservation settings) so guests' replies reach you. Replies go to the people you chose in Send Avante's emails to.

🛍️ Front store (your own booking page)

Not for Channel Properties (NightsBridge and other channel managers). They sell through hook and booking links.

What it is: a page that sells only your property, at go.avantetravel.co.za/stay/your-name. Guests see your photos, pick dates, see what's free and the price, and book and pay on the page. The booking comes in like any other booking (source "Front store"), and you and the guest both get an email.

You build the page in 9 steps. Next to the steps is the Live preview: it shows the page the way guests see it, and changes as you type. Guests see nothing new until you tap Save front store.

Quick start

  1. Tap Front store on the tab bar.
  2. Tap ✨ Fill in my store. It fills the empty boxes for you: headline, the line under it, highlights, a photo per unit and Google photos for things to do. It never changes what you typed.
  3. Look at the Live preview. On a phone, tap 👁 Preview at the bottom, and ✕ Close to go back.
  4. Change what you like in the steps below.
  5. Tap Save front store.
Tap a step to open it; the preview jumps to that part of the page. 📱 Phone and 💻 Computer above the preview show the page at phone or computer size. ⚠ Changes not saved yet means you still need to tap Save front store.

1. Switch on and link

  1. Check the Link name (for example dunes-hotel). Letters, numbers and dashes.
  2. Tick Front store switched on when the page looks right.
  3. Tap Save front store. It's live straight away.
  4. Tap View my front store to see it.

2. Top of the page

  1. Type the Headline (the big words on the photo), e.g. Your getaway in Keurboomstrand. Empty = your property name.
  2. Type the Line under the headline.
  3. Leave Price badge empty to show your lowest price a night (from Reservation settings), or type your own, e.g. From R1,200 a night.
  4. Optional: copy your Google stars into Stars (0 to 5) and your number of reviews into Number of reviews.
  5. Optional: paste the Link to your Google reviews.

3. Photos

  1. Tap Upload photo to add your own (they are made smaller automatically).
  2. Or type what to look for under Find photos on Google and tap Search Google. Tap Use on a photo you like.
  3. Photo already online, e.g. on your Avante listing? Paste its link in Add a photo by link and tap Add photo.
  4. Untick a photo to hide it. Tap ↑ to move it earlier. The first photo (★ Cover) is the big one at the top; the next five make the photo block under it.
  5. Tap ✥ Move on a photo to choose which part of it shows: drag it (or use the arrows), then tap Done.
  6. Back to automatic uses your photos, then Avante's.

4. Highlights

  1. Type up to 6 short highlights, e.g. Swimming pool, Sleeps 6, Pet friendly. Empty boxes don't show.

5. Your units

  1. Untick a unit that must not be on the page.
  2. Type its Beds, e.g. 1 queen, 2 singles.
  3. Type One line about it.
  4. Under Photo for this unit, tap a photo. The chosen one has a ✓ and a thick border. Tap it again to take it off.
The price on each unit card is the nightly price per unit in Channels → Reservation settings. No price there = no price on the card.

6. Things to do nearby

  1. Tick Show things to do on the page.
  2. Check Your area (things to do are found from here). Empty = automatic (your map pin, or your town in the location tree). To change it, type a few letters of your suburb or town and pick it from the list.
  3. Pick how far in Within (5 to 150 km). Guests can pick another distance on the page too.
  4. Untick one to hide it. Guests see 10 of the ones you leave ticked, picked at random on every visit, closest first.
  5. Under On your page now you see the 10 that show: 🔀 Random ones change on every visit, 📌 Kept ones stay.
  6. To keep one, tap Keep. To show another instead, tap Swap, pick it in Show this instead and tap Choose: it is then kept. Let it be random undoes it. You can keep up to 10.
  7. Photos fill in by themselves: first the activity's photos on the Avante map, then the shared photo library, and only if there is nothing there a Google photo (which then goes into the library for next time).
  8. Want another one? Tap Change photo. Pick one of the Photos on the Avante map or In the photo library, or type other words under Google photos and tap Search Google, then tap Use. Or tap Upload my own photo.
  9. Tap Move photo to choose which part of the photo shows. Drag the photo (or use the arrows), then tap Done.
  10. Remove my photo (or Back to the automatic photo) takes your photo off again.

7. What shows on the page

  1. Tick or untick Prices, Availability calendar and Your special offers.
  2. Type Book at least … days ahead (0 = guests can book for tonight).

8. Words and contact details

  1. If you like, type your own About the property (empty = your Avante description).
  2. Type the Phone, WhatsApp number and Email guests should use (empty = your Reservation settings). The email box takes several addresses, separated by a comma.

9. Guest page and room card (for guests who are staying)

  1. Type your Wi-Fi network, Wi-Fi password and Good to know (house info), like check-out time and gate code.
  2. Tap Save front store.
  3. Under Links and QR codes, tap 🖨️ Print room card.
  4. Tap 🖨️ Print this card. It prints on A5. Put one in every room.
  5. Guests scan the big QR for the guest page: WhatsApp, call, email, Wi-Fi, house info, directions and things to do. The small QR books their next stay.
You can also send guests the guest page link (Copy under Guest page), for example in your welcome message in Communication. The guest page link is secret: Wi-Fi and house info only show with it, never on the public front store. Worried it got out? Tap New guest link, then print new room cards (the old ones stop showing Wi-Fi).

Share it

  1. Under Links and QR codes (shows once the front store is switched on), tap Copy next to the front store link and paste it in your website, Instagram bio or WhatsApp status.
  2. Or tap Share on WhatsApp.
  3. For flyers and posters: tap Download QR. Guests scan it with their phone camera.
On a phone, guests book from the calendar: they pick dates under the calendar and tap Check availability for these dates, or tap Check availability in the bar at the bottom. The Book your stay box is not shown on phones (when the calendar is on). It only appears with the free units once the guest has checked.
The numbers under the QR codes show visits and bookings from your front store this month. Your minimum stay and calendar start date (Channels) also apply on the page. Special offers are hooks you manage yourself (Affiliate Marketing). In the preview, booking is switched off.
Bookings on the front store are real Avante bookings. To stop misuse, the page takes at most 10 online bookings a day (3 per email address) and stays of up to 30 nights; longer stays are asked to contact you. Switch the front store off (untick, Save front store) and the page and guest page stop working.

⚙️ Channels: settings and payments

Reservation settings

  1. Tap Channels.
  2. Type Your email (and on Avante reservations) and Phone on Avante reservations (required). Several email addresses? Separate them with a comma.
  3. Type your Minimum stay (nights).
  4. Under Calendar opens on, choose Today, or A set date and pick the date.
  5. Optional: type a nightly channel price per unit.
  6. Someone else handles your bookings and questions? Under Contact person for bookings and queries, type the Contact person, Contact phone and Contact email.
  7. Pick who gets Avante's emails in Send Avante's emails to: Both (your email and the contact person), Your email only or The contact person only.
  8. Tap Save reservation settings.

Avante sets you up in the hub as soon as you're on Avante. You only connect when you want to use it.

Your first login

  1. Tap Bookings. The list shows only bookings that came through Avante: your hook and booking links.
